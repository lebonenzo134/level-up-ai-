Questions

When can I use it?

We're opening access in small groups. People on the waitlist get in first.

How much will it cost?

There will be a free plan with a few checks per week. Premium is planned at around $7.99 a month for unlimited checks and your saved closet.

Does it work for any style?

Yes. You tell it the style you like, and advice follows that, from streetwear to smart casual.

Will it judge my body?

No. It looks at the clothes: colors, fit, proportions and the occasion. It never rates your looks.

What happens to my photos?

They're used for the check, then deleted. Nothing is shared or used to train AI models.

iPhone or Android?

Both. Early access starts in your phone's browser, so there's nothing to install.
